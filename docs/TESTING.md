# Validation status

Version: 0.4.0, local; automated checks on 2026-11-10. Of 0.4.0, only the WoW: Forever window and bar frames have been seen and accepted in game (2026-11-09/10); the rest of 0.4.0, and every other client, has not been seen in a game client yet. For 0.3.2, client metadata was rechecked against installed builds and UI sources on 2026-09-15, when publication was explicitly requested; this does not certify in-game coverage for every client.

## Automated

Placement tests cover nearest-free-position search, visible-frame collection, cooperative time limits, cancellation, scaled Blizzard action bars and expansion from the compact view. Routine audio refreshes preserve pending checks. Position tests (0.3.6) cover drags that start from the shown position, refreshes and events during a drag, drags ended by a view switch, Escape, hiding or a release outside the window, re-grabbing during an overlap check, the layout-cache opt-out, re-applying the saved position after loading and UIParent resizes, and audio events that no longer move the window. Since 0.3.7 they also cover UIParent scale changes by other addons: a direct `UIParent:SetScale` and the pixel-perfect sequence of a uiScale write followed by a lower scale. The bar is laid out once on the next frame, keeps its saved position and a pending overlap check, and no OnUpdate stays active while idle. Text tests (0.3.8) cover the wider value fields of the compact bar and fitting values, channel names, the title and the footer when text needs more room, as at small effective scales: after layouts and value changes, down to a minimum size, with and without `GetUnboundedStringWidth`. The removed in-game release notes no longer have runtime or layout tests. The final 0.3.2 package, including its smaller minimap logo, was confirmed in [Retail](curseforge/acceptance/v0.3.2-retail.md) and [TBC Anniversary](curseforge/acceptance/v0.3.2-anniversary.md) on 2026-09-15. MoP Classic and Classic were additionally confirmed for 0.3.2 on 2026-09-16 (see below). The full scale/resolution matrix still requires in-game inspection.

**1709 passing scenario checks** execute the actual addon in Lua 5.1 against bounded frame/CVar doubles. Seven configurations represent Retail, Mists Classic, TBC Anniversary, Classic Era, the WoW: Forever beta (project 1, interface 16001), the WoW: Forever release client (project `WOW_PROJECT_CAMELOT` = 18, interface 16001) and an unknown-client/global-API fallback; each runs with native and fallback dropdown menus. These are API simulations, not the game engine.

Theme tests (0.4.0, `tests/test_theme.lua`) run in all seven configurations. The client's look is Forever's only in the two Forever configurations; elsewhere it builds no Forever region and keeps the 0.3.8 colors. The tests apply the other look directly (`UI:ApplyTheme(name)`), since players cannot switch. A round trip `soundstone` → `forever` → `soundstone` with the mixer and options open restores every region exactly (texture, atlas, texcoords, colors, size, visibility, alpha, draw layer, blend mode and anchors), moves nothing and keeps the saved position. The plates' line and band lie inside the frames' bounds on an edge frame above the content that takes no mouse input, and the frames keep their 276/300-unit widths. Lines, wells and slider tracks measure one physical pixel over 45 scale combinations: physical heights 1080/1440/2160, client scales 0.53/0.64/0.7/1/1.15 and Soundstone 75/100/150%. Dividers take one pixel under the Forever look and their units again under the sprites. Further cases cover the client gold on titles and section labels with and without `NORMAL_FONT_COLOR`, the text and footer colors, wells, gear and Expand states and grip squares. The slider thumb takes the atlas `Minimal_SliderBar_Button` when the mocked `C_Texture.GetAtlasInfo` knows it and keeps the sprite with its 0.3.8 texcoords and size when it does not, or without `C_Texture`; under `soundstone` it is always the sprite. A switch during a drag and during a placement check sizes the lines and keeps both running. `/soundstone theme` with any value is an unknown command that changes nothing, the help text no longer lists it, and a saved `theme` from an unreleased build is dropped at load and on reload; the corrupted-settings case checks the same for a damaged value. A secret effective scale makes the lines fall back to one unit. In the Forever configurations the shared scenarios run under the Forever look; without the client's atlases in the double that is the flat stand-in, so they check the stand-in there.

Client-art tests (0.4.0) give the double Forever's atlas names (`Mock.foreverAtlases`, with stand-in sizes) and a reduced `NineSliceUtil`. In the two Forever configurations the mixer and options then wear the window border (anchored −4.4/−3.35 and +0.5/+0.35, ten levels above the window, no mouse) with the rock ground and the top streaks; the Baganator-style header buttons (32 × 22, twelve levels up, gold symbols); their own close buttons with the `RedButton-*` atlases; and the title strip as drag handle with the title on it. Rows sit 37 units apart, and the footer and the options' controls stay above the bottom band. The bar wears the same frame cut to its band: the layout's own top pieces from 5 units above the box to 3 below it, the bottom corners' lowest 16 units, the edges between them as NineSlice anchors them; the windows' ground and a short shade under the top band; its controls inside the band: gear and Expand as red 24 × 22 buttons that open the options and the mixer and show their tooltips, the sprite-look buttons hidden, 64-unit channels with their values 2 units before the next divider. Each piece the cut reads frames at exactly the height it needs and leaves the whole frame look flat one unit below it. Tracks use the three slider pieces at atlas height with the fill 4 units in, and the channel icons show the action-button frame, its highlight on hover and its pushed art while held. The frame controls switch views, open and close the options, hide Soundstone and show the same tooltips; Escape keeps its order. A round trip with the art, then without it, returns every sprite-layout region exactly, compared with the state before any client art was applied, in the expanded view with the options open and in the compact view: textures, colors, sizes, anchors, font sizes, texts and frame levels; everything the art added is hidden again and nothing moves. Without single pieces of the art (a window corner, a track piece, a pushed icon frame) only that piece falls back to the flat look. The icon frame drops its pushed art when hidden while held, after a release outside the game window and on an unavailable channel. A drag lifts the border and the controls above level 500. In the other five configurations the same atlases leave the flat look in place. The reload check confirms that the framed look comes up on Forever's first load and nowhere else.

Audio checks cover startup without writes, mute preservation, zero/100% boundaries, rounding, sliders and wheel steps, external changes, missing APIs, rejected/ignored/throwing writes, slash commands, bindings, minimap behavior, reload and corrupt saved settings. The audio matrix runs 192 switch/zero-volume transitions per configuration/backend. Additional cases cover effects/ambience/dialogue grouping, music-only activation while master is blocked, mixed external settings, positive-volume history, first-use fallbacks, readback, every write-failure position and failed rollback. Voice/pet/error-speech settings remain untouched. Synchronous CVar callbacks cannot display intermediate group states.

The existing geometry matrix covers 1080p/1440p, WoW scale 65/85/100%, addon scale 75/100/150% and both views. It checks inherited scale, all four screen corners, icon clearance, equal grip dots, dropdown alignment and pixel positioning. The new options matrix additionally tests four corners plus the center in every combination: matching left edges, 276-unit width, a four-unit gap, above/below selection, no overlap, screen containment, 256-unit dropdown width and restoration of the saved position. It verifies all three header click areas remain 20 × 20 with aligned tops. A dedicated case covers temporary repositioning when neither side initially has room, including a pending size change that is cancelled on close.

Header-control tests cover common normal/hover/pressed colors, right-button suppression, release/re-entry, mouse release outside, hide cleanup, tooltips, title clearance and order **hide → compact view → X**. Existing direct view/hide actions, layered Escape, shared position, minimap independence, pending-size cancellation and reload restoration remain covered. The compact button keeps its existing geometry and plate states.

Device cases include one restart per successful change, unchanged/rejected selections, unavailable APIs, restart failure, unplugged/reordered devices, stale index/name checks, external changes, long names, six-row scrolling, outside clicks, second clicks and menu closure on view changes. Native frames are simulated outside UIParent with independent menu-manager Escape ownership.

The reported native `SetFont` error was reproduced by rejecting even a method lookup on compositor-managed labels. The corrected code creates one shared Font object outside the initializer and uses `SetFontObject`, following the [menu guide](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source/live/Interface/AddOns/Blizzard_Menu/11_0_0_MenuImplementationGuide.lua) and [compositor restrictions](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Gethe/wow-ui-source/live/Interface/AddOns/Blizzard_Menu/Compositor.lua). Repeated opening, selection and regeneration are tested. The prior unrestricted FontString mock missed this bug.

Package validation checks TOC entries, XML, 24 runtime textures, real alpha, atlas agreement, ZIP integrity and one installable folder. HeaderHide, HeaderCompact and HeaderClose must have identical dimensions, byte-identical alpha silhouettes and matching outer-frame pixels. Their PNG previews come from the same export as the TGA files.

The scenario count counts test invocations, not every assertion inside the matrices. Approximate font metrics and API doubles cannot establish in-game font rendering, physical hit targets or audible device switching.
