# Soundstone 0.4.1 – Azeroth Volume Mixer

## What's changed

- **New name:** Soundstone is now **Soundstone – Azeroth Volume Mixer**, a name that says what it does. WoW's addon list, the Key Bindings menu and the tooltip of the mixer's title bar show it. The title bar itself shows "Soundstone – Volume Mixer": the full name does not fit there at any size.
- **Centered title:** In Retail and the Classic clients, the mixer's title now sits centered between the gear and the header buttons instead of being pushed against the hide button. WoW: Forever already centered it this way.
- **Small interface scales:** Where even the short title does not fit, the title bar shows "Soundstone" at its full size instead of the smallest one.

The addon folder, your settings, positions and volume levels, `/soundstone` and `/azeraudio` stay as they are; nothing is migrated. Everything else is as in [Soundstone 0.4.0](https://github.com/krebs3r/soundstone-azeroth-audio/releases/tag/v0.4.0).

## Installation and validation

Download **Soundstone-0.4.1.zip** from this release's assets and replace the **Soundstone** folder in your client's **Interface/AddOns** directory (WoW: Forever: **_forever_/Interface/AddOns**). Keep your **WTF** folder to preserve settings.
